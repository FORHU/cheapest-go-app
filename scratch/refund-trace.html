A Visa refund to a Philippine debit card is expected to post within five to ten business days. Both refunds are now well beyond that window. Because the original purchases settled as POS withdrawals rather than holds, each refund returns as a separate inbound credit rather than as a reversal of the original line, and will not appear against the original transaction.
